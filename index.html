    How LeagueSim works

    1. Roll

      Every open lane rolls a random champion who's actually played in that role.

    2. Lock one in

      Keep the roll you like best. The other lanes reroll, so pick your moment.

    3. Play it out

      Lanes are scored with real ranked win rates from this patch, plus team comp and bot lane duo synergy. Then the match plays out.
