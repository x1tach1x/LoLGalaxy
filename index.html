About LaneLock

LaneLock is a set of free daily puzzles for League of Legends players. In the Draft, five enemy champions are already locked in and you roll and lock a team to counter them. In Who wins the lane?, you see two champions in the same lane and pick the one that wins that matchup more often in ranked games.

Everyone gets the same puzzles each day, and they reset at midnight UTC. Results use real ranked win rates from the current patch. See how a draft is scored.
