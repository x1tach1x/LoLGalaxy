Who wins the game? #5

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Blue wins 82% of the time

Blue (Protect comp): Poppy, Amumu, Azir, Kai'Sa, Milio
Red (Engage comp): Ambessa, Graves, Corki, Tristana, Yuumi

  • Blue wins bot lane. Dead even in real games: Kai'Sa wins 50.3% vs Tristana (12,526 games).
  • Blue's protect comp beats Red's engage comp: peel and disengage stop the dive cold.
  • Blue wins the jungle. Amumu wins 51.9% vs Graves (893 games).

Game 2 (medium): Red wins 74% of the time

Blue (Poke comp): Nasus, Brand, Veigar, Tristana, Seraphine
Red (Engage comp): Olaf, Xin Zhao, Ahri, Yunara, Renata Glasc

  • Red wins the jungle. Brand is niche tier, Xin Zhao is A.
  • Red's engage comp beats Blue's poke comp: hard engage closes the gap before poke can wear them down.
  • Blue's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.

Game 3 (hard): Blue wins 66% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Jax, Maokai, Brand, Jinx, Karma
Red (Engage comp): Ambessa, Poppy, Syndra, Ashe, Tahm Kench

  • Blue wins bot lane. Jinx wins 52.4% vs Ashe (5,601 games).
  • Blue scales better: 2 late-game champions to 0.
  • Blue wins the jungle. Maokai is B tier, Poppy is niche.
