Who wins the game? #7

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Red wins 87% of the time

Blue (Poke comp): Yone, Karthus, Kassadin, Caitlyn, Zyra
Red (Engage comp): Sett, Naafiri, Xerath, Jhin, Maokai

  • Blue's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.
  • Red wins the jungle. Karthus is B tier, Naafiri is S.
  • Red wins top. Sett wins 53.4% vs Yone (3,120 games).

Game 2 (medium): Blue wins 75% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Darius, Sylas, Zoe, Aphelios, Pantheon
Red (Poke comp): Urgot, Nidalee, Syndra, Ezreal, Senna

  • Red's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.
  • Blue's engage comp beats Red's poke comp: hard engage closes the gap before poke can wear them down.
  • Blue wins top. Dead even in real games: Darius wins 49.6% vs Urgot (1,293 games).

Game 3 (hard): Blue wins 63% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Illaoi, Nidalee, Taliyah, Vayne, Galio
Red (Engage comp): Fiora, Rek'Sai, Ziggs, Caitlyn, Rakan

  • Blue wins mid. Taliyah is A tier, Ziggs is niche.
  • Red's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.
  • Blue wins the jungle. Nidalee is A tier, Rek'Sai is B.
