Who wins the game? #3

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Red wins 77% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Tryndamere, Diana, Annie, Kalista, Shaco
Red (Engage comp): Olaf, Lillia, Vex, Miss Fortune, Nami

  • Red wins bot lane. Miss Fortune wins 56.3% vs Kalista (535 games).
  • Red wins the jungle. Lillia wins 52.6% vs Diana (1,140 games).
  • Blue's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.

Game 2 (medium): Blue wins 69% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Sett, Qiyana, Ryze, Aphelios, Blitzcrank
Red (Engage comp): Tahm Kench, Malphite, Taliyah, Sivir, Brand

  • Blue wins bot lane. Blitzcrank wins 56.2% vs Brand (1,104 games).
  • Blue wins top. Sett wins 51.8% vs Tahm Kench (951 games).
