Who wins the game? #6

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Red wins 79% of the time

Blue (Poke comp): Teemo, Gwen, Hwei, Xayah, Poppy
Red (Engage comp): K'Sante, Taliyah, Xerath, Yasuo, Sona

  • Red wins the jungle. Gwen is niche tier, Taliyah is A.
  • Red's engage comp beats Blue's poke comp: hard engage closes the gap before poke can wear them down.
  • Red wins mid. Xerath wins 52.2% vs Hwei (932 games).

Game 2 (medium): Blue wins 69% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Sett, Lee Sin, Brand, Aphelios, Karma
Red (Engage comp): K'Sante, Kha'Zix, Mel, Kai'Sa, Neeko

  • Blue wins bot lane. Aphelios wins 53.2% vs Kai'Sa (12,472 games).
  • Blue wins top. Sett wins 52.4% vs K'Sante (1,773 games).
  • Blue is stronger early: 2 early-game champions to 0.

Game 3 (hard): Blue wins 63% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Volibear, Lee Sin, Fizz, Yunara, Poppy
Red (Engage comp): Teemo, Ekko, Vladimir, Tristana, Seraphine

  • Red's comp: damage leans heavily magic.
  • Blue is stronger early: 2 early-game champions to 0.
