Who wins the game? #8

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Blue wins 91% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Shen, Shaco, Malzahar, Draven, Janna
Red (Engage comp): Irelia, Karthus, Corki, Ashe, Pyke

  • Blue wins the jungle. Shaco is S tier, Karthus is B.
  • Red's comp: very little frontline to soak damage.
  • Blue wins mid. Malzahar is S tier, Corki is niche.

Game 2 (medium): Red wins 70% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Teemo, Poppy, Galio, Caitlyn, Lulu
Red (Engage comp): Singed, Zyra, Taliyah, Xayah, Nautilus

  • Red wins the jungle. Poppy is niche tier, Zyra is A.
  • Red wins mid. Taliyah wins 56.0% vs Galio (541 games).
  • Red wins bot lane. Xayah wins 51.7% vs Caitlyn (11,452 games).

Game 3 (hard): Blue wins 63% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Aatrox, Lillia, Azir, Kog'Maw, Nautilus
Red (Engage comp): Quinn, Trundle, Malzahar, Nilah, Pantheon

  • Blue wins the jungle. Lillia wins 58.9% vs Trundle (261 games).
  • Red's comp: damage leans heavily physical.
  • Blue wins top. Dead even in real games: Aatrox wins 49.4% vs Quinn (353 games).
