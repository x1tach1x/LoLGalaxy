Who wins the game? #4

Each game was simulated with LaneLock's match model, the same one that scores the Draft. The biggest reasons the favourite won:

Game 1 (easy): Blue wins 80% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Kled, Sejuani, Neeko, Miss Fortune, Rakan
Red (Engage comp): Illaoi, Amumu, Mel, Hwei, Nautilus

  • Blue wins bot lane. Rakan wins 53.8% vs Nautilus (5,699 games).
  • Blue has a combo: Sejuani holds enemies still for Miss Fortune's ultimate.
  • Red's comp: damage leans heavily magic.

Game 2 (medium): Blue wins 69% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Ornn, Udyr, Neeko, Kai'Sa, Seraphine
Red (Engage comp): Tahm Kench, Lillia, Katarina, Corki, Nami

  • Red's comp: almost all magic damage, so the enemy only needs magic resist.
  • Blue wins top. Ornn wins 55.4% vs Tahm Kench (602 games).
  • Blue scales better: 2 late-game champions to 0.

Game 3 (hard): Blue wins 64% of the time

Blue (Engage comp): Olaf, Talon, Yone, Zeri, Seraphine
Red (Poke comp): Illaoi, Maokai, Xerath, Aurelion Sol, Karma

  • Blue wins the jungle. Talon is S tier, Maokai is B.
  • Blue's engage comp beats Red's poke comp: hard engage closes the gap before poke can wear them down.
  • Blue wins mid. Yone is S tier, Xerath is A.
