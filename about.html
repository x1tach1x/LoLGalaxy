About LaneLock

LaneLock is a free daily drafting game for League of Legends players. You get a random enemy team and a few seconds of luck per lane. Your job is to turn that into a draft that wins.

How to play

  1. See the enemy team. All five enemy champions are revealed before you pick anything.
  2. Roll your lanes. Each of your open lanes rolls a random champion who's actually played in that role.
  3. Lock one in. Choose the roll you like most. Every other open lane rerolls, so you're always trading a sure thing now against a better roll later.
  4. Repeat until all five are locked. Then the match plays out, and you see who won each lane, how the team comps compared, and how the game unfolded.

Want the details of the scoring? Here's how LaneLock decides who wins.

Daily and unlimited

The daily draft is the same for everyone in the world. Everyone faces the same enemy team and starts from the same first rolls, so you can compare results with friends. A new daily starts at midnight UTC. After you finish, you'll see how many players won and lost that day.

Unlimited mode gives you a fresh random enemy team every game, as many times as you like.

Why it exists

Champion select is one of the most argued-about parts of League. Everyone has opinions about counters, comps and who should have picked what. LaneLock turns that into a quick puzzle you can play in a minute or two, built on real ranked statistics instead of guesswork, with an explanation for every result.

Questions

Is LaneLock free?
Yes. It's supported by a small number of ads.
Do I need an account?
No. Your stats and streak are saved in your browser. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, they start over.
Where does the matchup data come from?
Lane win rates come from LoLalytics (Emerald+ ranked solo queue, patch 26.22). Lane tiers come from public patch tier lists. Champion portraits come from Riot's Data Dragon.
Why did I lose a lane I thought I'd win?
Open the "Lane by lane" panel after a game. It lists the win rate or class matchup behind each lane's result, so you can see exactly what tipped it.
Is LaneLock made by Riot Games?
No. It's an independent fan project and isn't endorsed by Riot Games.
I found a bug or have an idea.
Email contact@lanelock.net. Feedback on matchups that feel wrong is especially welcome.
