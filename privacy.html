Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

LoL Galaxy (lolgalaxy.com) is a free fan site with games for League of Legends players. This page explains what information the site uses and why.

No accounts

You don't sign up or log in, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Game progress stored on your device

LeagueSim saves your stats (games played, win rate, streak) and your daily result in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, isn't sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Community stats

When you finish the daily LeagueSim draft, the site sends whether you won or lost to our stats counter (a Cloudflare Worker) so we can show how the community did that day. To count each player only once per day, the counter stores a one-way hash of your IP address combined with the date. Your actual IP address isn't stored, and the hash can't be turned back into it.

Advertising

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Other services the site loads

  • Champion images come from Riot Games' Data Dragon image service.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the community stats counter runs on Cloudflare. Both may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.

Each of these services handles requests under its own privacy policy.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the site: contact@lolgalaxy.com
