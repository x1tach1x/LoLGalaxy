How to play Who wins the lane?

You get five matchups a day, one in each lane. Each shows two champions who face each other in that lane. Pick the one that wins the matchup more often in ranked games. After each pick you see both win rates and how many games they're based on.

Where the answers come from

Every matchup uses real ranked solo queue results from Emerald rank and above on the current patch. Only matchups with enough games to be reliable are used, and close-to-even matchups are left out so every question has a real answer. In easy matchups the winner takes 54% or more of games, in medium 52 to 54%, and in hard 51 to 52%.

Tips

  • Ranged champions often beat melee champions in lane, because they can trade without taking damage back.
  • Champions that heal or sustain do well against poke, which relies on wearing the enemy down slowly.
  • A champion's overall strength matters too: a strong pick this patch wins many matchups it "shouldn't" on paper.

Want more? Endless mode keeps going until your first wrong answer. Past answers are in the answer archive.
