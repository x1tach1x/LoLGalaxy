LeagueSim

The enemy team is locked in. Each of your lanes rolls a random champion: lock one in and the rest reroll, until your whole team is set.

LeagueSim was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.

Lane matchups use real ranked win rates from LoLalytics (patch 26.22, Emerald+ solo queue) where there are enough games. Lane tiers follow public patch 26.22 tier lists; "niche" champions are playable in a lane but weren't ranked there. Team comps, duo fit and the play-by-play are a simplified model, so treat results as for fun, not as coaching.
